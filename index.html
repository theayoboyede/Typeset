Why pairing matters

A headline font sets the mood. A body font carries the reading. When they agree on rhythm but differ in shape, the page feels designed instead of assembled.

Contrast, not conflict

Try a serif headline over a clean sans body, or the other way around. Keep weights close to what each font does best, and let size and spacing do the rest.

The best pairings feel like one voice with two moods.
